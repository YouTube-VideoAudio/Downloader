Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

We keep things simple: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no selling of data. This page explains what little information is involved when you use the app and the website.

In short: the app works on your own phone. The video links you paste and the files you download are not sent to us.

1How the app works

When you paste a link, the app on your phone contacts the video platform directly (for example YouTube), gets the video details, and downloads the file to your device. This is done by the open-source tools yt-dlp and FFmpeg inside the app. We do not run a download server, so your links and files do not pass through us.

2What we collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or password. There are no accounts.
  • No payment details.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access. The app needs internet access to work.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies.

3What third parties may see

  • The video platform. It sees the requests made from your device, such as your IP address, as it would if you opened the video yourself. Its own privacy policy applies.
  • GitHub. This website and the app file are hosted on GitHub. When you visit the site, download the app, or when the app downloads updates for its tools, GitHub may log standard request data such as your IP address. GitHub’s privacy statement applies.
  • Thumbnails. The preview image is loaded from the video platform’s image servers, so your device contacts them directly when it is shown.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

4Files on your device

Downloaded files are saved in the Downloads/Video Downloader folder on your phone. The app also uses temporary files while it works, and removes them when it finishes. We cannot see or access any of these files. You can delete them yourself at any time, and uninstalling the app removes its own data.

5Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and the app safe. Please download the app only from this website, and avoid installing files from unknown sources.

6Children

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7Your choices

Because we do not collect personal information, there is usually nothing for us to delete. If you have a question or a request about your privacy, email us at youtubevideodownloader@atomicmail.io.

8Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest change. See also our Terms of Service.

9Contact

Privacy questions? Email youtubevideodownloader@atomicmail.io.
