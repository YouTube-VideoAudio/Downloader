Questions?

Is it free?

Yes. No signup, no payment and no ads.

Why do I need to install an APK?

Apps like this are not available on Google Play, so you install the APK directly. Android shows a warning for any app installed this way. Please download it only from this page.

Is it safe?

The app needs no account, runs on your phone and its source code is public. Install it only from this website, and only download content you have permission to use.

Which websites work?

YouTube and many other sites that yt-dlp supports, for example Vimeo, Dailymotion, Reddit and TikTok. Support varies and can break when a site changes. Adult sites, live cam sites, paid-subscription sites and DRM-protected streaming services are blocked.

Which formats are available?

MP4 in up to 1080p, depending on the video, and MP3 audio.

Does it work on iPhone or computer?

Not yet. The app needs Android 10 or newer, or the Legacy APK on Android 7.1 to 9.

Can I download anything?

Only download content that you own or have permission to use. Videos belong to their original creators. See our Terms and DMCA page.
